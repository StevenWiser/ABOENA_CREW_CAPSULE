# Handoff: build the ABEONA Crew Capsule engine and tanks in Onshape

Onshape document: https://cad.onshape.com/documents/ad9a6d4b75d67328be0a01a2/w/3973cd08c6897a85595c46d7/e/bb28d537a3204d397e59d9f9
Auth: env vars ONSHAPE_ACCESS_KEY / ONSHAPE_SECRET_KEY (HTTP Basic, access:secret). Units: mm.

Geometry sources (generated by scripts/s12_cad_geometry.py, do not change the design):
- cad/engine_profile.csv : x_mm (from throat, + toward exit), r_inner_mm (gas side), r_outer_mm, region
  (ablative = silica-phenolic liner + 2.5 mm Ti case, up to area ratio 6; C103_extension = 0.6 mm skirt)
- cad/layout.json : service module (4980 dia x 6000 long, z up from SM base), engine stations,
  MMH and NTO Ti-6Al-4V spheres (centre z, inner dia, wall), 4 helium COPV spheres, clearances.

Build list:
1. Engine: revolve the profile about the axis as separate parts: ablative liner, Ti case, C-103 extension;
   injector plate (20 mm, chamber dia) with 12 acoustic cavities; head/valve/gimbal stack as a simple cylinder.
2. Tanks: MMH and NTO spheres (shells, given wall), 4 COPV spheres at 45/135/225/315 deg.
3. Envelope: SM cylinder + 0.5 m aft cone as a transparent reference body; check no interference.
4. Place everything in the SM coordinate frame from layout.json; name parts clearly.
Report: (mass properties per part, interference check, screenshots) back to the user.
