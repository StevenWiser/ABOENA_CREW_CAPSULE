## Interference check

The check computed pairwise boolean intersections of all 11 hardware bodies (55 pairs) on copies in Onshape:

- **One interference:** the head/valve/gimbal stack and the NTO tank overlap by **19,417 mm³**. This is a spherical cap 2.16 mm deep.
  Cause: `s12_cad_geometry.py` places the tank at `z_o = z_gimbal + Do/2` using the *inner* diameter, so the
  2.16 mm wall reaches below the gimbal plane. Fix in the design data: add the wall thickness, i.e. `z_o = z_gimbal + Do/2 + t_o`.
  That raises the MMH top by 2.16 mm, which still fits inside the current 6.27 mm top clearance. I did not change the model
  (the handoff says not to change the design).
- Every other pair has zero overlap. The liner touches the case, the extension, and the injector plate, and the plate touches the head
  stack, all face to face (distance 0, overlap 0). The NTO-to-MMH gap is 95.7 mm. The tank-to-COPV gaps are 592.5 mm (NTO) and 593.0 mm (MMH). COPV-to-wall is 50 mm.
- Against the SM envelope (cylinder plus 0.5 m aft cone), every part is inside except the C-103 extension. 3.27 L of it sits outside, which is
  expected because the exit plane is 2010 mm below the SM base and the cone is only 500 mm deep.

Assumption: acoustic cavities are modelled as 12 axial Ø16 mm bores, 59.4 mm deep from the injector face, on a 100 mm pitch radius.
The bores pass through the plate into the head stack. The design data gives only their number and depth.
