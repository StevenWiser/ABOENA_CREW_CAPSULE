# ABEONA Crew Capsule: Onshape build and RocketCEA check

Document: [ABOENA_Crew_Capsule](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/ad9a6d4b75d67328be0a01a2/w/3973cd08c6897a85595c46d7/e/bb28d537a3204d397e59d9f9)
Part Studio 1 has one feature, **ABEONA engine and tanks**. It is defined in the Feature Studio *ABEONA build FS*
and generated by `onshape/build_onshape.py` from `abeona/cad/engine_profile.csv` and `layout.json`.
Units are mm, in the SM frame: the origin is at the SM base on the axis, with +Z up. Run `onshape/verify_onshape.py` to regenerate the numbers below.

## Model contents (v3)

**New in v3:**
- **Capsule + launch escape system:** a reference shell only (20 mm display wall, no material, 35 % opacity, left out of the mass and interference checks).
  The profile is traced from the PDR slide-7 drawing at 4.98 m across and 11.8 m from the SM top to the tip (tip at z = 17.82 m).
  It covers the bullet fairing, the neck, a 0.74 m tower and a rounded nose. The drawing's ".41" dimension lands on the nose (about 0.41 m wide there).
  It does not match the drawn tower, which measures about 0.74 m wide.
- **Smooth engine:** the liner, case and C-103 extension are now revolved from fitted splines through the profile stations, not polylines.
  Each one is a single smooth body of revolution, with no faceted rings.
  The extension is the gas-side spline surface thickened by a constant 0.6 mm (33.2 kg, against 32.5 kg calculated by hand).
  It is trimmed where it meets the liner end face. The liner, case and extension stay separate parts because they are different materials.

**From v2** (PDR review slide 7, plus your requests):

- **Tanks match the PDR drawing:** 2.6 m ID spheres (9.203 m³ each). The wall is scaled from s08 to 2.14 mm, keeping the same MEOP and allowable stress.
  Equal volumes are correct here: at O/F 1.65, the NTO density (1440 kg/m³) and MMH density (875 kg/m³) give 9.08 and 9.06 m³ of propellant (PDR 1.2.2-01/02).
  **Consequence:** ullage falls to 1.3 % (NTO) and 1.6 % (MMH). The analysis package sized the tanks for 5 % ullage, which gives a 2.63 m diameter.
  1.3 % is below the usual 3–5 %. Either go back to 2.63 m or offload about 3 % of the propellant.
- **The tanks now sit on their outer surfaces.** This removes the v1 head-stack/NTO overlap (19.4 cm³).
  Stack: NTO centre z = 1928 mm, MMH centre z = 4632 mm, 100 mm gap between the tanks, 65 mm to the SM top.
- **The COPVs are helium pressurant bottles.** Four 31 MPa COPVs feed a regulator, which holds both tank ullages at 1.33 MPa. That is a regulated
  pressure-fed system, so there are no pumps. The 87 kg of helium is sized in s08 for the cold adiabatic case. The bottles now sit at z = 3280 mm.
  Each is a 20 mm shell with lumped density, so the cut view shows them hollow.
- **New piping (all 321 stainless).** Each line meets its tank, regulator or engine inlet with zero overlap and at most a 0.12 mm gap:

| Line | Size (OD × wall) | Basis | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| NTO feed | 1.5 in × 0.065 in (34.8 mm ID) | s08: 4.95 kg/s at 3.6 m/s, 64 kPa line loss | NTO lower dome (r = 400 mm) → down → +X side of the valve stack at z = 500 mm |
| MMH feed | 1.5 in × 0.065 in | s08: 3.00 kg/s at 3.6 m/s, 50 kPa line loss | MMH bottom pole → between the tanks → down at x = −1450 mm (outside the NTO tank, between the COPVs) → −X side of the valve stack |
| He HP manifold | 0.375 in × 0.065 in | 31 MPa, hoop stress 90 MPa, factor 5.7 on ultimate; ~22 m/s | COPV top outlets → ring (r = 1961 mm, z = 3819 mm) → regulator |
| He LP lines | 0.75 in × 0.049 in | 1.33 MPa; ~16 m/s He at the 3.4 L/s ullage displacement rate per tank | regulator (+Y, r = 1700 mm) → each tank's upper dome, 60° from the top pole |

  The regulator/check-valve panel is a lumped 10 kg block. Not modelled: valves, filters, gimbal flex sections in the feed lines, PMDs, and supports.
- **Cut view toggle:** edit the feature **ABEONA engine and tanks** and tick **Cut view (remove −Y half)**. Every body is cut on the XZ plane,
  which runs through both feed lines. Untick it before reading mass properties. Onshape's own *Section view* tool (View menu) also works without editing the feature.

## Nozzle extension wall check

- **Pressure is not what sizes it.** The gas pressure is 19.9 kPa at the attach station (ε 6, Mach 3.0) and falls to 0.44 kPa at the exit.
  Hoop stress is therefore 5.7 MPa at the attach station and 0.5 MPa at the exit. C-103 keeps far more strength than that at its 1,389 K peak (s06, 1,600 K limit).
- **Temperature does not depend on thickness.** A radiation-cooled skirt runs at radiation-equilibrium temperature whatever its thickness,
  so a thicker wall only adds mass. Thin walls are normal for this kind of skirt.
- **What actually sizes it:** stiffness against vibration, gimbal and handling loads, and the attach flange.
  The report covers these with a +25 % mass allowance for stiffening rings, flange and coating (40.7 kg against 32.5 kg bare), but the CAD does not show them.
  If you want the hardware to look more robust: taper the wall from about 1.0 mm at the attach flange to 0.6 mm at the exit,
  and add 2–3 hat-section stiffening rings. Both are drawable without changing the thermal design.


A two-bottle helium layout (2 × 1.206 m COPVs) was tried and fitted with 376 mm tank clearance. It was reverted to the four-bottle baseline at the user's request.

## Interference check (v3)

The check computed pairwise boolean intersections of all 17 hardware bodies (136 pairs) on copies in Onshape.
**There is no interference.** The largest overlap of any pair is 4×10⁻⁵ mm³, which is numerical noise at tangent contacts. The connected parts meet with gaps between 0 and 0.12 mm.
The 0.12 mm gap is at the MMH outlet stub: its flat end sits against the curved bottom dome.
The NTO-to-MMH tank gap is 95.7 mm, and each COPV is 601 mm from both tanks.
Against the SM envelope, only the C-103 extension is outside (3.27 L), which is expected because the exit is 2010 mm below the SM base.

Assumption: the acoustic cavities are 12 axial Ø16 mm bores, 59.4 mm deep, on a 100 mm pitch radius. The design data gives only their number and depth.

## RocketCEA re-run of the design point (`analysis/rocketcea_point.py`, RocketCEA 1.2.3)

The *report basis* case uses the report's thermo.inp heats of formation and 293.15 K propellants (s04 dh_f, dh_o).
